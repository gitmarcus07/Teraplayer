# TeraPlayer — Watch & Download TeraBox Videos Online

**Live demo: https://teraplayer.in**

TeraPlayer is a free online tool that opens public TeraBox share links right in your
browser. Paste a link to **watch TeraBox videos online**, preview files, or **download
TeraBox videos** — no login, no app install, works on phone and computer.

![TeraPlayer — open TeraBox links in your browser](https://teraplayer.in/logo.png)

## Features

- ▶️ **TeraBox video player** — stream shared videos instantly in HD
- ⬇️ **TeraBox video downloader** — save videos and files to any device
- 📁 **Folder browser** — open multi-file shares, search, sort, download as ZIP
- 🎞️ **Quality picker** — choose resolution when available
- 🔒 **Password-link support** — unlock protected shares in-app
- 📱 **Mobile-first** — fully responsive, no app needed
- 🌙 **Dark mode** — light, dark, and system themes
- 🌍 **8 languages** — English, Hindi, Indonesian, Bengali, Urdu, Arabic, Nepali, Spanish

## How to use

1. Copy any **public** TeraBox share link
2. Paste it into the box at [teraplayer.in](https://teraplayer.in)
3. Press **Watch** — stream instantly, or hit **Download** to save it

No account. No TeraBox app. Only public links — sign-in links and private
shares aren't supported.

## Supported link types

- Single video and file shares
- Multi-file folders (browse + ZIP download)
- Password-protected public shares
- All major TeraBox mirror domains (`terabox.com`, `1024terabox.com`, `nephobox.com`, …)

## FAQ

**Is TeraPlayer free?**
Yes — 100% free, unlimited links, no hidden charges.

**Do I need the TeraBox app?**
No. Everything happens in your browser.

**Do you store my files or links?**
No. Links are resolved in real time and streamed directly — nothing is kept
on our servers. See the [Privacy Policy](https://teraplayer.in/privacy).

**Why doesn't my link work?**
The share may be private, expired, or deleted. Check the
[Help Center](https://teraplayer.in/help-center) for fixes.

## Tech stack

React + Tailwind (frontend) · FastAPI + MongoDB (backend) · Cloudflare Workers
(optional extraction relay). Self-hostable with Docker/Render — see `render.yaml`.
